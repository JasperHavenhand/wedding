Getting there

Travel & where to stay

We're right in Liverpool's Georgian Quarter, an easy trip from the station and city centre. Here's how to reach us and where to stay.

By train

Liverpool Lime Street is the main station, about a 12-minute walk or a short taxi from Hope Street. Direct services run from Manchester, Preston, Leeds and London.

By car & parking

There's no parking at the hotel itself, but the Q-Park on Hope Street is a two-minute walk and open 24 hours.

Where to stay

Hope Street Hotel has rooms on site — book direct and mention our wedding for the guest rate.

There are also plenty of hotels within a short walk in the city centre if you'd like more options.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Can I bring a plus-one? +

Your invitation will name everyone we've got a space for — if a guest is included, it'll say so on your RSVP.

Are children welcome? +

We love your little ones, but we've decided on an adults-only celebration so everyone can relax. Immediate family excepted.

What about dietary requirements? +

Absolutely — there's a spot on the RSVP form to tell us. We'll have great vegetarian options as standard.

Is there a gift list? +

No list and no gifts needed — your company on the day is the only thing we're after.

What's the weather likely to be? +

Mid-March in Liverpool can be bright but brisk, so bring a coat for arriving and leaving. Everything on the day is indoors, so you'll be warm once you're in.

One last thing

Will you join us?

Please let us know by 31 January 2027 so we can finalise numbers. It takes two minutes.
